# Module 3: Managing Security Risks & Frameworks

## 📌 Overview
This module focused on foundational risk management frameworks, security audits, and threat detection mechanisms used to safeguard organization assets and maintain operational resilience.

## 🔑 Key Concepts & Takeaways

### 1. Risk Management Frameworks (NIST CSF & RMF)
* Learned the core functions of the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)**:
  * **Identify:** Asset management, business environment, and governance.
  * **Protect:** Access control, data security, and protective technology.
  * **Detect:** Anomaly detection, security monitoring, and continuous monitoring.
  * **Respond:** Incident response planning, communications, and mitigation.
  * **Recover:** Recovery planning, improvements, and resilience restoration.
* Understood the role of the **NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF)** in establishing structured risk assessment lifecycles for organizational assets.

### 2. Threat Analysis & Vulnerability Management
* Examined common attack vectors and web application vulnerabilities outlined in the **OWASP Top 10**.
* Applied security concepts including the **CIA Triad** (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) and the principle of **Least Privilege** to minimize exposure risks.

### 3. Security Audits & SIEM Fundamentals
* Analyzed the structure of **Security Audits** to evaluate organizational compliance against internal policies and regulatory standards.
* Introduced to **SIEM (Security Information and Event Management)** tools for log aggregation, centralized monitoring, and security event detection.

## 🛠️ Practical Skills Applied
- Conducting basic security risk assessments based on NIST guidelines.
- Evaluating control effectiveness against identified threat scenarios.
- Reviewing security audit checklists to identify gaps in access control and data handling.
