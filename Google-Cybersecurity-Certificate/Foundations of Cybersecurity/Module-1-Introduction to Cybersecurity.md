# Module 1 Notes: Introduction to Cybersecurity & Core Concepts

## Overview
This module introduces the fundamentals of information security, the evolving threat landscape, the role of entry-level security analysts, and why cybersecurity is critical for modern organizations.

## Key Security Concepts
* **Definition of Cybersecurity:** The practice of protecting systems, networks, programs, and data from digital attacks, unauthorized access, or destruction.
* **The CIA Triad:**
  * **Confidentiality:** Ensuring data is accessible only to authorized users.
  * **Integrity:** Safeguarding the accuracy and completeness of information and processing methods.
  * **Availability:** Ensuring authorized users have timely, reliable access to information and resources.

## Role of an Entry-Level Cybersecurity Analyst
* **Monitoring & Detection:** Scanning networks and logs for suspicious activities or security alerts.
* **Incident Support:** Assisting in mitigating threats, documenting security incidents, and gathering evidence.
* **Compliance & Auditing:** Ensuring internal policies and external regulations are followed.
* **Security Awareness:** Educating staff on best practices like spot-checking phishing emails and password hygiene.

## Core Takeaways
* **Historic Context & Evolution:** Cyber threats have expanded from basic viruses to organized cybercrime and state-sponsored attacks.
* **Security Culture:** Effective defense combines technology, process management, and continuous user education.
