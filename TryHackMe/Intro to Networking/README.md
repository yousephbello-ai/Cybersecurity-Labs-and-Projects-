# TryHackMe - Intro to Networking | Write-up

Completed the **Intro to Networking** room on TryHackMe, covering fundamental networking concepts essential for cybersecurity analysis and penetration testing.

## 📌 Key Concepts Covered
- **OSI & TCP/IP Models:** Understanding the 7 layers of communication and how data encapsulates/decapsulates across networks.
- **Core Protocols:** Overview of TCP, UDP, IP, ICMP, DNS, and HTTP/HTTPS.
- **Network Troubleshooting & Reconnaissance:** Practical usage of utilities like `ping`, `traceroute`, `ipconfig`/`ifconfig`, and `netstat`.
- **Ports & Services:** Identifying standard listening ports (e.g., HTTP on 80, SSH on 22, DNS on 53) and their security implications.

## 🛠️ Practical Takeaways
- Gained hands-on familiarity with how packets traverse local networks versus the internet.
- Strengthened foundational knowledge needed for network scanning with Nmap and traffic analysis with Wireshark.
