## Overview
This module covers how organizations use **security frameworks and controls** to reduce risk, the **CIA triad**, NIST frameworks, and **security ethics**.

---

## 1. Security Frameworks & Controls

**Security Framework**  
A set of guidelines and best practices that help organizations manage cybersecurity risk in a structured way.

**Security Controls**  
The specific safeguards used to protect assets.

### Types of Controls
| Type              | Description                          | Examples |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------|----------|
| **Technical**     | Technology-based                     | Firewalls, encryption, MFA, antivirus |
| **Administrative**| Policies and people                  | Security policies, training, risk assessments |
| **Physical**      | Tangible protections                 | Locks, CCTV, badge readers, guards |

**Defense-in-depth** = using multiple layers of controls so that if one fails, others still protect the organization.

---

## 2. The CIA Triad

The core model of information security:

- **Confidentiality** → Only authorized people can access the information  
- **Integrity** → Information is accurate and has not been improperly changed  
- **Availability** → Systems and data are accessible when needed  

Every security decision should support one or more of these three principles.

---

## 3. NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

NIST provides free frameworks used by many organizations.

**Five Core Functions:**
1. **Identify** – Understand assets and risks  
2. **Protect** – Put safeguards in place  
3. **Detect** – Spot cybersecurity events quickly  
4. **Respond** – Take action on incidents  
5. **Recover** – Restore systems after an incident  

---

## 4. Ethics in Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity professionals must act ethically. Key principles:

- Protect **confidentiality** and **privacy**
- Follow laws and regulations
- Handle PII and sensitive data carefully
- Stay honest and unbiased

Leaving sensitive information exposed (e.g. unlocked patient records) violates confidentiality, privacy, and legal obligations.

---

## Key Terms
- **CIA Triad** – Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability  
- **Security Control** – A safeguard that reduces risk  
- **NIST CSF** – Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover  
- **Defense-in-depth** – Multiple layers of security
